Our story

A small café with a big table.

We opened Bean Hub in 2019 with one espresso machine, a second-hand sofa and a simple idea: the best cafés feel like someone saved you a seat. Today we still roast in small batches, source milk from a family dairy forty minutes away and keep the long table free for study groups, first dates and Friday pitch rehearsals.

Whether you have ten minutes between lectures or an afternoon of deadlines, there is a corner here for you. Order at the bar, choose a seat and stay as long as you need.
